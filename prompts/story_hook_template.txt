STRUCTURE = STORY HOOK (this is the experiment; follow the order exactly):
1. SITUATION (scenes 1-2, the first 2 seconds of speech): open on ONE concrete, real situation taken from the source below, as a short scene with a person and an action, in plain words. 3. EXPLANATION (scenes 4-8): what the study found and the one explanation the authors give, in plain words, with the limits from the FACT BASE.
4. PRACTICAL ACTION (one scene): one light everyday action, no health advice.
5. CTA (last scene): a spoken follow call to action to a PUBLIC episode, exactly this line: "<CTA LINE>". The episode named must already be public on YouTube. Never "Watch next" to a clip without a link.

FACT BASE: you may use ONLY the facts below, nothing else, and nothing may be added, strengthened or explained further.
<NUMBERED FACTS: (1) the source, said plainly as "a <year> study" or "researchers"; no journal name needed; (2) the concrete situation exactly as the source describes it, nothing invented; (3) the finding; (4) the authors' explanation, worded as "one explanation is"; (5) limits; (6) practical action>
Allowed numbers: only those listed in the FACT BASE.
FORBIDDEN: causal wording the study does not support ("causes", "proves", "makes you"), any invented detail of the situation (names, places, dates, objects) that is not in the source, percentages, medical advice, universal generalisations ("everyone", "always").
